07 / Strategy

Liquidity, structure, and aggressively boring execution

Higher-timeframe bias, lower-timeframe trigger

Weekly and daily give direction, the 4H gives key levels, the 15m or 5m gives the trigger. A lower-timeframe setup without daily context is not traded — that single filter deletes about 60% of what looks good. Break of structure, change of character, then entry on the order block or fair value gap retest, stop beyond the structure that failed.

Liquidity sweeps beat indicator crossovers

No RSI or MACD religion. The levels everybody watches — session highs and lows, previous day high and low, round numbers — are where the manipulation happens. Sweep, displacement, reclaim: those are my three repeatable setups. An oscillator confirms divergence; it never buys an entry.

Partial booking and trail maths

50% off at 1R with the stop moved to break-even, 25% off at 2R, the runner aimed at 3.5–4R. This is what smooths the equity curve — and on a funded account “smooth” translates to “challenge passed”, while in a managed pool it means “the investor did not panic and leave”.

Instruments, and why these

XAUUSD (gold) — deepest retail liquidity, cleanest ranges, double stop distance on news.
EURUSD / GBPUSD — tight spreads and the most reliable respect for structure.
US100 / US30 — explosive inside session killzones, deadly outside them.
BTC / ETH perps — swing timeframes only, weekend flat rule enforced.

GUIDE 01 · ADULT CONTENT

Pornography and XXX content: what compulsive use actually costs

Free, infinite, novelty-on-tap is a completely new variable in human history. Nothing moral is required to notice that a habit this easy can quietly re-price the things that used to reward you — patience, boredom tolerance, real relationships, sleep, and the ability to sit with an uncomfortable feeling without numbing it.

What is happening mechanically

Every click supplies a fresh novelty hit, so the brain never has to work for reward; over weeks, ordinary stimulation starts to feel flat and the content has to get more extreme, more specific or more frequent to produce the same effect. That escalation is not a character flaw, it is habituation — the exact same curve that makes a punter double the stake. Most people who describe “loss of interest in real intimacy”, difficulty concentrating at work, late-night sleep loss and a shame-and-repeat loop are describing habituation plus sleep deprivation, which is very treatable once both are addressed.

When it has crossed the line

  • You intend to look once and it becomes an hour, repeatedly, and you can name the cost.
  • It is the default response to stress, loneliness, boredom or rejection — the four classic triggers.
  • Escalation: what worked six months ago now does nothing.
  • Secrets, deleted histories, or guilt that follows into your relationships and work.
  • Time you promised to someone else, or sleep you cannot afford to lose.

A 14-day baseline, not a lifelong vow

Vows fail because they rely on willpower in the moment of weakest willpower. Friction works. For fourteen days: no adult content on the phone or bedroom devices (block at the router and at the DNS, not just in the browser), one logged note of what you felt in the ten minutes before each urge, exercise or a shower as the replacement action, phone outside the bedroom at night, and one person told. Two weeks of data beats two years of promising — and if the urges stay intense and uncontrollable after a clean fortnight, that is a reason to see a therapist or counsellor, not a reason to shame yourself.

Any content involving a child is a crime, not a habit. Report it and do not download or share it: IWF (UK), NCMEC CyberTipline and the INHOPE hotline network handle reports anonymously, in most countries.

GUIDE 02 · BETTING & SPECULATION

Gambling and betting: the mechanics engineered to keep you playing

A betting app is a slot machine with your own favourite sport attached. The maths is designed so the house keeps a margin over time; the design is designed so you keep playing long enough for that margin to collect.

Four features doing the work

  • Variable-ratio reinforcement. Unpredictable payoffs are the single most habit-forming schedule known. A fixed salary never produces this pull; a free bet does.
  • Near misses. Two legs of a four-fold landing feels “so close” and increases the urge to re-stake. It is a loss, presented as encouragement.
  • Losses disguised as wins. Cash-out offers, bonuses, and “wait for the next in-play market” turn a bleeding balance into a feeling of activity.
  • Frictionless deposit, frictionless stakes. Saved cards, one-tap bets, and live odds on a phone remove every natural pause a bookmaker's shop once forced on you.

Why “strategies”, tipsters and VIP signals mostly fail

Betting systems that raise the stake after a loss (martingale variants) do not change expected value; they only change how long the account survives before a losing run arrives, and a losing run always arrives. Tipster groups sell survivorship — the wins are posted, the losses are deleted, and the “recovery plan” is the next product in the funnel. Ask one question: if the edge were real and large, why would it be sold to you for a monthly fee? Compare the scam patterns and the risk rules on this site — the difference between a plan and a gamble is that a plan writes its loss limit down first.

What actually reduces harm, in order of effectiveness

  • Self-exclude, do not “cut down”. Register with national self-exclusion schemes and the operator lists, then remove the apps — access removal beats intention.
  • Install a gambling-specific blocker (Gamban, GamBlock or similar) on every device including the partner's, plus the router-level block.
  • Ring-fence money: bank gambling-block toggle, one spending account only, bills paid on the day income arrives, cards held by someone else if needed.
  • Tell one person today. Secrecy is the fuel; disclosure is the first structural fix.
  • Replace the hour. Betting peaks at night and after stress — book a gym session, a walk, a study block, anything with a start and an end.
  • Get clinical help if there is debt, borrowing, or continued betting despite harm. This is a recognised compulsive behavioural disorder with real treatments, not a weakness.

Debt created by betting should be told to someone and to a free debt-advice service the same week. Interest compounds faster than recovery does, and a written plan stops the panic-decisions that create the second hole.

GUIDE 03 · ATTENTION & PLATFORM SAFETY

Unsafe and addictive platforms: the attention business, and how to opt out

Feeds, streaks and autoplay are not accidents. A social or short-video platform earns advertising revenue per minute of attention, so its engineering team is paid to remove every reason you might stop. Judging yourself for losing to a well-funded team of designers is a category error; change the environment instead.

Recognition list — the dark-pattern toolkit

  • Infinite scroll and autoplay with no natural stopping point.
  • Streaks, snap scores and daily quests that convert usage into fear of loss.
  • Vague “someone mentioned you” notifications engineered to be unanswerable without opening the app.
  • Predatory in-app purchases, loot boxes, “spin to win” gamification — including inside finance and betting apps.
  • Anonymous money channels, DM-first contact, and any platform that pushes you to a closed chat before you have verified a person.
  • Copy-trading bots, doubling schemes and “guaranteed daily profit” groups: same psychological hook, this time attached to your savings — see the risk scanner.

Eighteen-month rule for children, and a rule for you

For households: put age gates on the app store account rather than the device, enable Screen Time or Google Family Link, set router schedules so Wi-Fi sleeps at night, keep charging outside bedrooms, and — the part that actually works — agree the same rules for adults. For yourself: no phone for the first 30 minutes of the day and none in the bedroom, one feed deleted (not reduced) for 30 days, greyscale in the evening, all non-human notifications permanently off, and a fixed 20-minute daily slot for anything infinite.

Sextortion — the fastest-growing harm, and the fix is counter-intuitive

A stranger or a fake account moves a conversation to video, then threatens to send recorded images to your contacts unless you pay. The playbook is short and firm: do not pay a single coin (payment marks you as a payer and increases demands), do not delete the evidence, capture usernames and wallet addresses, cut all contact and lock every account privacy setting, tell one real person immediately, and report to police plus the platform's abuse channel and a national body. Victims are not criminals and no reputable service will shame you for reporting.

A useful test for any platform or app: could I stop it at a natural boundary? Feeds with no end, chat with no identity, money with no withdrawal proof — three no's means leave.

GUIDE 04 · FRAUD & FINANCIAL SCAMS

Fraudulent websites and online scams: recognition, and the first sixty minutes

Every scam shares one architecture: an outsized promise, manufactured urgency, and payment in a direction that cannot be reversed. Learn those three and most of the internet's traps go quiet.

The eleven shapes worth memorising

  • Advance-fee “release” scams: tax, customs, clearance or verification charges before your money arrives.
  • Recovery rooms: someone offers to trace money you already lost, for a fee. Same crew, second collection.
  • Pig butchering: weeks of friendly chat, then a beautiful fake exchange where the balance can never be withdrawn.
  • Task and like-for-pay scams: small real payouts for a week, then a required deposit to unlock the big balance.
  • Clone broker or clone prop firm: real logo, real regulator number, a slightly different domain.
  • Fake KYC “verification deposit”, fake wallet-draining approvals, and malicious browser extensions that rewrite pasted addresses.
  • Crypto doubling, HYIP “2% daily”, Ponzi grids, matrix schemes and referral-mandatory “mining rigs”.
  • Phishing for a trading password, then using your access to blow the account deliberately for the payout dispute.
  • Investment livestreams with a paid audience, pump groups, and “signal” channels with no verifiable statement.
  • Job offers requiring you to buy equipment, training or a “starter kit”.
  • Anything where the person you are dealing with refuses to be recorded, documented or verified.

The first sixty minutes after you are scammed

  • Bank or card issuer: freeze the card, dispute the transaction, request a recall on the wire — speed is the only lever you have.
  • Accounts: change passwords, kill all active sessions, turn on an app or hardware second factor, revoke API keys and wallet approvals.
  • Evidence: export receipts, chat logs, wallet addresses, domain names and screenshots into one folder before anything disappears.
  • Report: your national cyber-crime or consumer-protection authority, the platform's abuse channel, and (if a regulated firm was impersonated) that firm and its regulator.
  • Tell someone: a scam works by isolating you; disclosure is damage control and it also protects the next person.

Do not pay to “unlock”, do not hire a “recovery hacker”, and do not accept a second investment that “makes you whole”. All three are the same scam wearing a different mask.

Library · Field Notes

Short reads for the questions people actually type at 2 a.m.

Seven practical essays — no scare tactics, no product to sell, no explicit material. Each one ends with what to do next, today.

The loss is what behavioural economists call a sunk cost: it cannot be recovered by any bet you are about to place, and the urge to “win it back” is the mechanism the industry is designed to harvest. Every chasing story ends the same way — a bigger hole and a smaller sense of self.

Do these five things in this order: (1) self-exclude and delete the apps today, while the resolve is real; (2) put a gambling block on the bank app and hand your cards to someone for 30 days; (3) total the loss once, write it down, and stop recalculating it — a number you refuse to look at grows; (4) tell one person the true figure out loud; (5) book a free session with a helpline or counsellor and treat the debt with a proper plan, because repayment schedules exist precisely so that a bad year does not become a bad decade. The money is recoverable over years. The habit is not recoverable if you leave it running.

Related: gambling mechanics guide · free helplines · emergency fund rule

Be accurate rather than alarmed, because alarm is easy to dismiss and accuracy is not. There is no credible evidence that adult content causes brain damage, and there is plenty of clinical experience showing that compulsive use can become a coping routine that crowds out sleep, real intimacy, patience with boredom, and self-respect. Habituation and escalation are ordinary learning processes — the same reason a song you loved in loop form gets stale.

Two honest conclusions follow. First, the useful measure is not how many times but how much choice is involved: can you stop easily, on a plan, without negotiation? Second, “reboot” communities often oversell a miracle while underselling the boring parts that do work: sleep repair, exercise, deleting the easy access route, therapy when shame or a mood disorder is present, and time. Fourteen days of clean data plus one conversation with a professional will teach you more about your own pattern than a forum thread ever will.

Related: full guide · dopamine detox, explained

Trading and gambling share the variable-reward loop; they differ in one thing only — whether you have an edge and whether you are allowed to act on it. These five settings turn one into the other, and they are the exact rules this desk runs accounts under.

1. Size is decided by a formula before the session, never during it. 2. The stop loss is placed with the entry, so the exit is not a decision you make while losing. 3. A daily loss cap ends the day — three losses and the terminal closes, no exceptions for conviction. 4. News events are traded flat, because a spike is a coin flip with spread. 5. Every trade is written in a journal with its rationale, so hope cannot be mistaken for a plan afterwards. Break any one of these five and your “strategy” is a betting slip with a chart attached — see the risk matrix.

Related: risk rules · risk of ruin · 30-day roadmap

A single browser extension is theatre — it is disabled in eleven seconds by the person who wants it off. Layers are what work, because each one has to be defeated separately and usually none of them are defeated at 02:00 when judgement is worst.

Start at the network: change your router's DNS to a filtering resolver (OpenDNS Family Shield, or Cloudflare's family filter) so every device on the Wi-Fi, including guests, loses access to the biggest domain categories. Add a device-level blocker (Cold Turkey, Freedom, or the built-in Screen Time / Digital Wellbeing limits) and set it with a random password you give to someone else. For betting specifically, install a gambling blocker and register the national self-exclusion, because new domains appear weekly. Then close the money route with the bank's gambling-block toggle and a separate spending account. Finally, for children: app-store age gates, Family Link, and the same rules applied to the adults, which is the only version kids accept.

Confrontation about shame produces concealment; confrontation about structure produces change. That is the whole strategy. Do not litigate the morality of betting — the person already knows, and shame is the fuel of the loop.

Practical sequence: pick a calm, time-boxed moment and describe specific observed facts (“four times last week, the statement shows this”), state the impact on you plainly, then make one concrete joint request — self-exclusion, a bank block, or one appointment with a free helpline. Separate the finances immediately for your own protection: your savings, your name, your credit. Do not repay debts quietly; that is the mechanism that keeps the behaviour affordable. Offer to attend the appointment. Set one boundary you will keep even if nothing changes, and get your own support — Gam-Anon and similar family groups exist because this is exhausting and isolating, and it is not your fault.

Related: helplines · gambling guide

You cannot detox a neurotransmitter, and “dopamine fasting” as sold online is not a neuroscience protocol — dopamine is also what you feel when you finish a task, greet a friend or drink water. What the trend gets right, accidentally, is boredom tolerance. Compulsive use is nearly always a flight from an aversive state: fatigue, loneliness, anxiety, an unfinished hard task.

So the useful version is behavioural, not biochemical: for one defined window, remove the super-normal stimuli (infinite feeds, adult content, betting, short video) and leave everything else alone — including the boring work you were avoiding. Expect restlessness on days two and three; that is withdrawal, not proof that you need the app. Schedule the replacement before you start, because an empty hour is a relapse with good intentions. Keep one daily low-stimulation block permanently: it is the cheapest way to make normal life feel rewarding again.

Sextortion is a volume business, not a personal vendetta, and it works almost entirely on panic. Which means the correct response is procedural and boring — and the person on the other side is betting everything on you skipping it.

Do this, in order: pay nothing, ever, because the first payment marks you as a source and the demands multiply; do not delete anything — screenshot the chat, the profile, the wallet address or payment link, and the email that sent the threat; go offline on that account, set every privacy setting to maximum, and block the contact; tell one real person out loud, today, because secrecy is the leverage; report it to your police cyber unit or national body and to the platform, and (in India) cybercrime.gov.in or 1930, in Pakistan FIA 1121. If a minor is involved, report it immediately to a child-protection line — that changes the case from your problem to a crime being prevented.

People also ask

DIRECT ANSWERS · NO SIGN-UP · NO PRODUCT
Is trading a form of gambling?
It can become one. Trading holds a genuine edge when expectancy is positive, risk per trade is fixed, and stops are placed before entry. It becomes gambling the moment size is chosen by feeling, losses are chased, or the account is needed to pay bills. The line is not the instrument — it is whether the decision was planned and recorded in advance.
How do I block gambling and adult sites on every device at home?
Use layers: a filtering resolver on the router (OpenDNS Family Shield or Cloudflare 1.1.1.3 for Families), an app blocker on each device (Cold Turkey, Freedom) with a random password held by someone else, Screen Time or Family Link for children, and the bank's gambling block so deposits cannot happen on impulse. See the toolkit table.
How do I know if my betting or porn use has become a problem?
Watch for loss of choice rather than loss of control: continuing despite harm you can name, hiding time or money, restlessness when it is unavailable, borrowing or skipping bills to fund it, and promises to yourself that keep breaking. If two or more apply, a free conversation with a counsellor or peer group is worth more than another week of willpower.
What should I do in the first hour after an online scam?
Freeze the card and call the bank about a chargeback, change passwords and enable a second factor, export every receipt, chat log and wallet address as evidence, report to your national cyber-crime authority, then warn others on the same platform. Never pay a second fee to any “recovery” service — that is the same scam's second half.
